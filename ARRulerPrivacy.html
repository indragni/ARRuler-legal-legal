Privacy Policy

AR Ruler
Published by Indragni Solutions
Effective date: March 31, 2026

This policy describes how we handle information in connection with the iOS app AR Ruler (“the App”). If you use the App, you agree to this policy. We may update it; the effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

1. Summary

AR Ruler uses your device’s camera and ARKit to show augmented-reality measurements. Measurement data and preferences you save are stored on your device unless you choose to export or share content. We do not require an account for core features. The free version may show ads and use standard app diagnostics as described below. Optional Pro features are purchased through Apple.

2. Camera and AR processing

The App needs camera access to capture the live view used for AR plane detection and measurements. Video from the camera is processed on your device for AR; we do not upload your camera feed to our servers for routine operation. Apple may process AR-related data according to your device settings and Apple’s policies.

3. Information stored on your device

Saved measurements, presets, display preferences, and similar data remain in the App’s storage on your iPhone or iPad until you delete them or remove the App. Unless you explicitly export or share content, that data is not uploaded to our servers for routine operation.

4. Advertising (free version)

The free tier may display advertisements through Google’s Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) or similar providers Apple allows. Ad networks may collect or receive identifiers and usage-related data to measure delivery, limit fraud, and personalize or limit ad frequency, according to their own policies and your device settings. See Google’s privacy documentation and iOS Settings → Privacy (including App Tracking Transparency where applicable).

5. Diagnostics and performance

We may use platform or third-party tools that collect crash data, performance data, and limited usage or product interaction information to keep the App reliable. Details in App Store privacy labels reflect the SDKs enabled in a given build.

6. Purchases

Pro unlock and restores are processed by Apple In-App Purchase. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple provides transaction identifiers needed to validate entitlement. Use Restore Purchases in the App’s settings if you reinstall or change devices on the same Apple ID.

Refund requests

If you ask Apple for a refund of a purchase made in the App, Apple may ask us for information about that purchase. We may then tell Apple: that the purchase was delivered and works, that a free version or free trial was available, how long ago the purchase was made, and whether we think the refund should be granted. We do not share your name, contact details or anything you create in the App. Apple makes the final decision. If you do not want us to share this information, email sandeep.bhandari@yahoo.co.in with your Apple order ID (shown on your Apple receipt) and we will not share it for that purchase.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 to collect their personal information knowingly. If you believe we have inadvertently collected a child’s data, contact us via our support page and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Security and retention

On-device data remains until you delete the App or clear its data through iOS. Data held by Apple, ad partners, or analytics providers is retained according to their policies. Use a device passcode and current iOS versions to help protect your device.

9. Your choices

  • Deny camera access in iOS Settings if you do not want AR features (the App may not function without it).
  • Upgrade to Pro to remove in-app advertising (subject to the current feature set).
  • Manage tracking and privacy in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the App to remove locally stored data (export first if the App offers export).

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through our Support page or the options listed on indragnisolutions.com.

This document supports the App Store listing for AR Ruler. It does not replace Apple’s terms or the privacy practices of Apple, Google, or other third-party SDKs integrated in the App.
