AR Ruler
Indragni Solutions

AR Ruler helps you measure real-world distances and sizes using augmented reality on iPhone and iPad. Grant camera access when prompted so ARKit can detect surfaces and show measurements.

Need more help? Contact options are listed on indragnisolutions.com. Include your iOS version, device model, and steps to reproduce; screenshots or screen recordings help.

AR not working or “move device”

  • Use the app in a well-lit area with visible texture on floors or tables (plain white surfaces can be harder to detect).
  • Move the device slowly so ARKit can map the scene.
  • Restart the App and ensure no other app is blocking the camera.

Pro purchase and Restore

  • Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase through Apple.
  • If Pro features do not appear after purchase, open the App → Settings (or the purchase screen) → Restore Purchases.
  • Use the same Apple ID you used to buy Pro, and ensure you are online when restoring.
